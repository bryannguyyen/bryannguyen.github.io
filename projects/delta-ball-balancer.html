Delta Robot Ball Balancer

Vision-based delta robot using OpenCV tracking, inverse kinematics, and PID control.

Overview: Built a ball-balancing platform actuated by a 3-servo delta robot. A camera tracks ball position in real time, and a Python control loop computes position error, runs PID control, solves delta inverse kinematics, and sends commands to an Arduino over serial to drive the servos.

Mechanical Design

  • 3-DOF delta robot platform driven by three servo actuated arms.

  • Designed for quick access to servos and easy reassembly during debugging.

Control & Software

  • Vision: OpenCV ball detection + mapping to the platform coordinate frame.
  • Control: PID loop on X/Y position error with output limits to prevent saturation.
  • Kinematics: Delta robot inverse kinematics to convert platform commands into joint angles.
  • Interface: Python to Arduino serial communication for real time servo control.

Implementation note: control updates and serial writes are rate limited to keep motion smooth and reduce oscillation.

Challenges & Iterations

  • Calibration: mapping camera coordinates to platform frame required consistent lighting and camera placement.
  • Latency: video processing + serial writes introduced delay; reduced with rate control and simplified image pipeline.
  • Tuning: PID gains adjusted to reduce overshoot and oscillation while maintaining responsiveness.
  • Mechanics: improved joint fit and alignment to reduce backlash that amplified control oscillations.
